PitchDelay for macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon)

1. Copy PitchDelay.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   and/or PitchDelay.vst3    to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/

2. The plugin is signed ad hoc, not notarized. Remove the download quarantine:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/PitchDelay.component
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/PitchDelay.vst3

3. Logic Pro: quit Logic. If PitchDelay does not show up, open
   Settings > Plug-in Manager, select PitchDelay and click "Reset & Rescan Selection".

PitchDelay.app is a standalone version for quick testing.
