PitchDelay for Windows (x64)

1. Copy the PitchDelay.vst3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
2. Rescan plugins in your DAW.

The build is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn when you open the zip or the
standalone PitchDelay.exe: choose "More info" > "Run anyway". PitchDelay.exe is a standalone
version for quick testing.
